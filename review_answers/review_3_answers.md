### Comment 5
> Please check that your many abbreviations are properly introduced. I stumbled over 'NFID' on page 6, which is not defined.

**Answer:**
Thank you for pointing this out. The abbreviation NFDI (German National Research Data Infrastructure) was previously only explained in a footnote. It is now introduced in the main text. We have also checked the entire manuscript and now introduce all abbreviations at their first use, specifically:

- RSE (Research Software Engineering) is now also introduced in the main text, not only in the abstract
- NFDI (German National Research Data Infrastructure)
- DFG (German Research Foundation)
- FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable)
- HIFIS (Helmholtz Federated IT Services)
- DLR (German Aerospace Center)
- UX (user experience) and UI (user interface)
- co-PI (co-principal investigator)
- FTE (full-time equivalent)

In addition, we replaced the abbreviations that were only used once.
